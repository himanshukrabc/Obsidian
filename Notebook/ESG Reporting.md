### Greenhouse Gas Protocols

GHG calculator is a major component of the project.  
CO2,CH4,N2O,fluorinated gasses

International standard for calculating GHG emissions. ⇒ has a corporate guidelines.

Types of emissions:  
1) Scope 1 ⇒ comes directly from company like vehicles , buildings owned by company.  
2) Scope 2 ⇒ emissions due to Electr/other resources used by your company  
3) Scope 3 ⇒ all the emissions that your company is directly/indirectly responsible for. Eg - purchased goods& services, flights, commuting etc.

This leads to double counting. Your scope3 will be someone’s scope 1.

SCOPE 3 is mainly not reported.
